The Weasley Clock

A grandfather clock rebuilt after the family clock in the Harry Potter books. Six hands show how far each person is from home, and six round screens beside the dial loop a short video of each person.

  • Design notes (PDF)Inspiration, the distance-to-angle mapping, the hollow-shaft motor stack, screens, parts and the to-do list.
  • 3D modelRotate and zoom the case, dial, hands, screens and motor stack.
  • Hardware planArchitecture, parts by module, costs, build phases and sourcing.
  • Circuit diagramPin-level wiring for the controller, six motor drivers and six display nodes.
